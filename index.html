ℹ About Mineclowter

Minecraft tools that just work

Mineclowter brings the calculators and guides Minecraft players search for into one fast, pixel-styled site: XP and enchantment costs, potion durations, Nether portal coordinates, a seed map with slime chunks, commands, color codes and PvP settings. No downloads, no accounts.

❓ FAQ
What is Mineclowter?

Mineclowter is a free collection of Minecraft tools by Hazik: calculators, converters, a seed map, a command generator and settings guides.

Is Mineclowter free?

Yes. Every tool is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser on desktop and mobile.

Does it support Java and Bedrock?

Most tools work for both editions. Tools that are edition-specific, like the slime chunk finder (Java) and add-on checker (Bedrock), say so on the page.
