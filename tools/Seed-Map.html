Seed Map

Tool
✋ Drag to move · scroll / pinch to zoom · click a marker to copy /tp

Structures (untick to hide)

Nearest to map center

Seed · Java Edition 1.21 placement rules. Structure and slime-chunk positions are calculated exactly from the seed. Biomes are not drawn and biome conditions are not checked, so a marker can be a spot where the structure did not generate. Strongholds are approximate (±100 blocks) because their final spot depends on biomes. Bedrock uses different rules.

About

Minecraft seed map with villages, strongholds, temples, monuments, mansions, ancient cities and slime chunks. Drag, zoom and hide any structure. It runs in your browser with no downloads and no sign-up.

FAQ
What does the Seed Map do?

Minecraft seed map with villages, strongholds, temples, monuments, mansions, ancient cities and slime chunks. Drag, zoom and hide any structure.

Is the Seed Map free?

Yes. It is free, needs no sign-up and runs in your browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, on phones, tablets and desktops.

Is the seed map accurate?

Structure and slime chunk positions are calculated with Java Edition rules. Biomes are not drawn and biome conditions are not checked, so some markers may not exist in-game. Strongholds are approximate.
